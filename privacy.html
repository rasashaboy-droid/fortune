Fortune House (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use this website, why we collect it and how we keep it safe.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact details you send us: name, phone number, email or messenger handle.
  • Booking details: dates, number of guests, apartment preferences and messages.
  • Technical data: browser type, device and pages visited, collected through cookies.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your requests and prepare apartment selections.
  • To process and confirm bookings with property owners.
  • To improve the website and our service.

3. Sharing

We share your data only with property owners and service partners when it is needed to complete your booking, and with authorities when required by law. We never sell personal data.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on secure servers and kept only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time by writing to hello@fortunehouse.example.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the website work and analytics cookies to understand how it is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Prices, availability and photos on this website are for information and may change. A booking is confirmed only after written confirmation from Fortune House.
