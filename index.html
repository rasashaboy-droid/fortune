Why Fortune House

What you get with every booking

The same six things whether you stay three nights or three months. They are part of the booking terms.

  • Photos that match

    Our manager visits every home before it is listed and checks it against the photos. If something on arrival differs, we move you to an equal or better home at our cost.

  • The full price upfront

    Pick dates and you see the total with cleaning and service fee. Electricity, water and Wi-Fi are already in it, so there is no meter reading at check-out.

  • A reply within 10 minutes

    A manager in your city answers on WhatsApp, Telegram or by phone at any hour, in English, Russian or Thai.

  • Monthly rates

    For stays of 28 nights or more, most owners give 20–40% off the nightly price. Ask your manager for the monthly rate before you book.

  • Check-in at any hour

    You get the door code on the day of arrival, or a manager meets you in person, including after night flights.

  • Cleaning and transfers

    Hotel linen and towels, a mid-stay clean for stays over a week, and an airport pickup or a scooter booked through your manager.
