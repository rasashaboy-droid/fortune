How it works

9 weeks from first call to first payout

That is for a ready unit. Off-plan purchases follow the developer’s timeline, and we take over at handover.

  1. Week 1

    Call and shortlist

    Budget, goals and timing. Within a few days you get 5–8 units with a forecast for each one.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Viewings and checks

    In person or by video call. Our lawyers check the title deed, the foreign quota, the licence and the building rules.

  3. Week 4

    Transfer

    Ownership is registered at the Land Office, with you there or through a power of attorney.

  4. Weeks 5–8

    Furniture, photos, listings and a smart lock. We send you a walkthrough video when it is done.

  5. Week 9

    First guests

    Bookings open. Your first statement and payout arrive on the 5th of the following month.

FAQ

Buying and owning as a foreigner

A condominium unit, yes, freehold in your own name, as long as foreign owners hold no more than 49% of the building’s floor area and the money is transferred from abroad. Land can’t be owned by a foreigner, so houses and villas are usually bought on a registered 30-year lease or through a Thai company. Our partner lawyers explain which structure suits you before you sign anything.

The Land Office charges a 2% transfer fee on the appraised value, which buyer and seller often split. Specific business tax of 3.3% or stamp duty of 0.5%, plus withholding tax, are normally the seller’s. For a buyer, 1–2% of the price usually covers it, plus a fixed fee for legal due diligence that our lawyers agree with you upfront.

Stays under 30 days need a hotel licence, and many condo buildings forbid them in their rules. We take short-stay guests only in buildings and villas where that is allowed and licensed. Anywhere else we rent month by month. We check this before you buy, not after.

Rent earned in Thailand is subject to Thai income tax. For owners who live abroad, 15% is usually withheld before the payout unless a tax treaty sets a lower rate. We withhold and file it and send you the certificates, so you can claim credit at home.

Yes. With managed rental you block your dates in the owner account. Book them early for December–February, because that is when the unit earns the most. With the fixed model, owner stays are up to three weeks a year between May and October.
